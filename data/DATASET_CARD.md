# Dataset Card — Real Estate Valuation

## Source
- **Dataset:** Real Estate Valuation
- **Publisher:** UCI Machine Learning Repository
- **Official URL:** https://archive.ics.uci.edu/dataset/477/real+estate+valuation+data+set
- **License:** CC BY 4.0, as listed by UCI
- **Geographic coverage:** Sindian District, New Taipei City, Taiwan
- **Records:** 414
- **Predictive features:** 6
- **Target:** House price per unit area

## Intended Use
Educational machine-learning experimentation, regression evaluation, data versioning, experiment tracking, and MLOps pipeline development.

## Limitations
The dataset is relatively small and represents a specific geographic market and period. Results should not be interpreted as current property valuations or as evidence of accuracy in other locations.
